## Selection policy
Use image sources with explicit public-domain or Creative Commons terms. Search-result images without a clear license are not approved for deployment. Prefer Bangladesh/Chattogram context where licensing is clear; otherwise use clearly licensed generic technical imagery and label it as illustrative rather than client project evidence.
