# Civil Solution Website — Design Direction

## Three initial approaches

### Theme Name: Warm Technical Atelier
Very Brief Intro: A warm, editorial engineering identity using cream paper tones, structural brown, turquoise accents, measured grids, and tactile project storytelling. It feels local, credible, and human without looking old-fashioned.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Coastal Survey Modernism
Very Brief Intro: A bright, airy direction inspired by Chattogram’s coastal light, using pale blue, sand, and precise survey geometry with generous open space. It feels calm and contemporary, but risks diluting the existing logo identity.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Structural Night Lab
Very Brief Intro: A dark engineering-lab aesthetic with blueprint lines, luminous turquoise, and orange instrument accents. It feels technical and cinematic, but is less aligned with the warm visiting-card palette.
Probability: 0.08

## Selected approach: Warm Technical Atelier

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial modernism fused with architectural drafting culture: warm paper-like surfaces, precise rules, offset compositions, and restrained material depth.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence before spectacle:** Projects, services, and process should feel documented and useful rather than over-promotional.
2. **Warm precision:** Cream and brown make the consultancy approachable; turquoise and orange create measured technical energy.
3. **Editorial hierarchy:** Asymmetric layouts, strong section labels, generous whitespace, and intentional typography should guide scanning.
4. **Contact-ready clarity:** Every page should make the next action obvious without becoming sales-heavy.

### Color Philosophy
Use the visiting card’s cream background as a spacious canvas, structural brown as the authority color, turquoise as the ownable action color, orange as a controlled warning/attention mark, and blue only for communication cues. The palette should feel like a technical drawing folder brought into a modern studio—not a loud construction marketplace.

### Layout Paradigm
Use offset editorial compositions rather than a centered stack: a left-aligned section rail, angled or clipped technical motifs, asymmetric hero split, project cards that alternate image and text emphasis, and narrow “field note” labels that give the page a surveyed rhythm.

### Signature Elements
1. A thin drafting rule with an orange datum tick that repeats across section headers.
2. Cream cards with subtle blueprint-grid texture and brown edge details.
3. Turquoise “field note” badges and circular measurement markers around project imagery.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like inspecting a drawing set: hover reveals metadata, project cards open a focused case-study panel, navigation underlines draw from left to right, and buttons press with a small physical response. Keep motion quiet and purposeful.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for hover, focus, nav drawer, and cards. Reveal section content with short upward fades and stagger only major groups. Use transform and opacity only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion by removing entrance motion and keeping state changes instant.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for display headings and labels, paired with **DM Sans** for body copy and interface text. Headlines should be compact, left-aligned, and use deliberate line breaks. Metadata should be uppercase with generous tracking. Body copy should remain 16–18px with comfortable line height.

### Brand Essence
Civil Solution is a Chattogram-based civil and structural engineering consultancy for clients who need clear technical evidence before making construction decisions. Personality: **grounded, exacting, responsive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and confident without inflated promises. CTAs are practical and specific. Microcopy explains what happens next.

Example lines:
- “Start with the condition. Build from the evidence.”
- “Tell us what the site is asking.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied CS structural mark as the primary symbol, paired with a clean English wordmark lockup and the original Bengali name when space allows. Do not redraw or distort the supplied mark. Use the symbol in the header, mobile menu, footer, and favicon treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**Civil Turquoise — #8CCFC5**. It owns the action layer of the brand: buttons, active states, survey markers, and small highlights.

## Implementation guardrails

- Do not use purple gradients, generic glassmorphism, or a centered SaaS hero.
- Keep cream, white, brown, and turquoise dominant; orange and blue remain accents.
- Do not invent testimonials, credentials, project outcomes, or hard metrics.
- Mark portfolio examples as sample content until verified by the client.
- Keep real logo assets outside the project directory and reference them through the approved asset URL workflow.

## Style Decisions

- **Drafting motif rule accepted:** Every major section must carry a purposeful cue such as a datum tick, section rail, coordinate label, measurement marker, or field-note badge.
- **Service index rule accepted:** The services area should read as an editorial technical index, not an equal-feature card grid; alternate card emphasis and add field-note metadata.
- **Project imagery rule accepted:** Use varied documentary evidence imagery and annotated sample-draft treatments instead of repeating generic construction crops.
- **Brand lockup rule accepted:** Give the supplied CS structural mark more presence and include the Bengali brand name in secondary footer/contact placements.
- **Typography rule accepted:** Strengthen Space Grotesk display contrast and reserve DM Sans for explanatory copy and interface details.
