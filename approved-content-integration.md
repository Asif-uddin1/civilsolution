# Approved Content Integration

## Company facts

The homepage now presents the client-approved facts inside the About Us section, immediately after the company introduction and before the Civil Solution signature. The facts are: established in 2017, approximately 10 team members, more than 40 site assignments, more than 30 clients served, and coverage across Chattogram and surrounding areas.

The block is bilingual. English mode uses `Established`, `Approx. team members`, `Site assignments`, `Clients served`, and `Chattogram`; Bangla mode uses the corresponding Bengali labels and `চট্টগ্রাম`.

## DEA

Detailed Engineering Assessment is available as a bilingual service card and dedicated route at `/services/detailed-engineering-assessment`. Its copy explains that DEA evaluates structural condition, strength, visible defects, safety considerations, and repair or retrofitting requirements. The exact scope remains subject to site information and an agreed technical brief.

## Project stories

Six work-only profiles are available in `client/src/content/home-content.json`. Four use client-authorized Civil Solution Facebook imagery and are tagged `AUTHORIZED WORK PROFILE`. Two are labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE SERVICE PROFILE` and explain Soil Test and DEA workflows without claiming a private client assignment.

No project story publishes client names, addresses, identities, dates, private documents, measurements, costs, testimonials, or unsupported outcomes. The homepage project lead includes a confidentiality note stating that private project identities, technical documents, and site images are not published without permission.
