# SEO implementation notes

Google Search Central states that SEO helps search engines understand a site, but no tactic guarantees a first-place ranking. Sustainable visibility depends on crawlability, useful original content, clear titles and descriptions, descriptive URLs, internal links, and ongoing measurement in Search Console.

The project will implement a practical foundation: page-specific title and description metadata, canonical URLs, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, JSON-LD for Civil Solution as a local professional service and its service catalog, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, visible bilingual content signals, and consistent Chattogram/Bangladesh service language. The implementation will not invent reviews, ratings, awards, addresses, opening hours, or performance claims.
