
## Visiting-card crop findings, tiles 1–2

## Visiting-card crop findings, tile 3 and comparison

Tile 3 clearly shows the right-side Bengali labels: **কনস্ট্রাকশন কেমিক্যাল** (Construction Chemicals), **রি-বার ফিক্সিং** (Re-bar Fixing), **রেট্রো-ফিটিং** (Retro-fitting/Retrofitting), **গ্রাউট এন্ড মাইক্রো কংক্রিট** (Grout and Micro Concrete), and **ল্যান্ড স্ক্যাপিং** (Landscaping). Across all three crops, the card contains ten printed services: Soil Test, Digital Land Survey, Integrity Test, Pile Load Test, Core Cutting and Scanning, Construction Chemicals, Re-bar Fixing, Retrofitting, Grout and Micro Concrete, and Landscaping.

Comparison with the current website catalog shows seven card services represented directly or through the merged soil service: Soil Testing & Investigation, Digital Land Survey, Core Cutting & Concrete Scanning, Construction Chemicals, Retrofitting, Grout & Micro Concrete, and Landscape & Gardening. Three card services are not currently represented as dedicated website services: **Integrity Test**, **Pile Load Test**, and **Re-bar Fixing**. The website also contains expanded services not printed on the card, including Survey & Mapping, Detailed Engineering Assessment, and Earthwork Boring.
